# Cardea landing page image prompts

These prompts define one coherent generated-image family for the landing page. They are atmospheric illustrations, not substitutes for real Cardea product UI, diagrams, or evidence.

## Reference lock

- Primary composition reference: `attachments/1folN1/image.png`, for editorial hierarchy, restrained classical imagery, paper grain, and product reveal below the opening copy.
- Asset-family reference: `attachments/NXiJuC/image.png`, for bone-white negative space, pale blue celestial atmosphere, Cardea beside a threshold, and imagery concentrated near a section edge.
- Supporting reference: `attachments/nYKlNC/image.png`, for wide classical scale and environmental storytelling.
- Cardea identity reference: `attachments/driNOu/Cardea.png`, for her profile, orbital braid, doorway, blue disc, and coral hinge.

## Shared visual language

- Warm bone paper ground `#F4F0E8`, Cardea blue `#445CFF`, hinge coral `#FF6B4A`, orbital black `#11110F`.
- Classical Roman forms interpreted through Japanese transit graphics and precise editorial composition.
- Screenprint, risograph, and fresco-like pigment texture, with visible paper tooth and controlled ink variation.
- Cardea remains calm, protective, intelligent, and monumental. Do not make her photorealistic or glamorous.
- No text, logos, product UI, fake screenshots, fake customers, watermarks, AI sparkles, glossy 3D, stock-photo lighting, purple gradients, or generic futuristic holograms.
- Preserve clean negative space for live HTML typography and real product components.
- Generate desktop and mobile crops separately after a direction is approved. Do not rely on automatic center cropping.

## 1. Hero: The threshold opens

Placement: first viewport background, with headline in the upper-left or upper-center and the real Cardea canvas entering from the lower 60 percent.

Desktop target: 16:10 landscape, 2400 x 1500 or larger. Keep the upper 38 percent quiet.

Prompt:

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: Cardea landing-page hero atmosphere behind real product UI
Primary request: Create a monumental editorial scene about crossing from human intent into coordinated action. Cardea stands in calm side profile near the lower-left, facing a tall open Roman arch at lower-center. A single orbital-black line leaves her braided hair, passes through a small hinge-coral orb, and continues through the doorway toward a distant world. Beyond the arch, faint Phoenix desert geometry transitions into San Francisco fog, hills, and restrained architectural silhouettes, suggesting a relocation without becoming a travel poster.
Composition: wide landscape; reserve the upper 38 percent as nearly empty warm-bone paper for live headline and navigation; concentrate Cardea, the arch, and atmosphere across the lower 60 percent; leave the center-bottom calm enough for a real product canvas overlay
Style: classical fresco distilled through Japanese transit-poster composition, Swiss editorial restraint, pale screenprint pigment, subtle paper tooth, quiet monumental scale
Palette: warm bone #F4F0E8, Cardea blue #445CFF used as pale and saturated ink, one hinge-coral #FF6B4A focal point, sparse orbital black #11110F
Lighting/mood: dawn through coastal fog, hopeful and intelligent, not mystical fantasy
Constraints: no text, no logo, no interface, no literal map pins, no airplane, no Golden Gate Bridge postcard composition, no fake UI, no watermark
Avoid: AI sparkles, cyberpunk, glossy 3D, generic SaaS gradient, photorealistic person, crowded clouds, fantasy goddess costume
```

## 2. Mechanism: One mission becomes a living system

Placement: first major section after the hero, paired with real animated mission nodes and flowing connectors.

Desktop target: 3:2 landscape. Primary forms should sit on the right two-thirds so copy can occupy the left.

Prompt:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Cardea landing-page mechanism section atmosphere
Primary request: Depict one central classical threshold opening into five connected architectural spaces at different depths, representing housing, travel, moving, furniture, and utilities without using literal icons. Thin orbital-black paths travel between the spaces, each carrying one small pulse of Cardea blue or hinge coral. When one distant doorway closes, adjacent paths visibly bend and reroute toward another open threshold, expressing live replanning and dependency.
Composition: asymmetric editorial panorama; leave the left 34 percent mostly empty for live copy; place the central arch near the middle and the branching spaces toward the right; strong depth but generous breathing room
Style: architectural etching mixed with transit-map clarity, screenprinted on warm bone stock, precise lines softened by tactile ink
Palette: warm bone, pale and vivid Cardea blue, sparse orbital black, exactly three small coral pulse points
Constraints: the image is atmosphere and metaphor only; real mission nodes and UI will be overlaid separately; no text, labels, arrows, interface cards, logos, or watermark
Avoid: flowchart infographic, tangled spaghetti lines, circuitry, neon electricity, fantasy ruins, photorealistic architecture, generic network visualization
```

## 3. Memory: What Cardea carries forward

Placement: memory section, behind or beside real interactive memory notes clustered on the canvas.

Desktop target: 4:3 or square detail crop, with object cluster low-right and quiet space upper-left.

Prompt:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Cardea landing-page memory section key image
Primary request: Create a tactile archive of personal memory as a cluster of elegant small transit cards, folded notes, fabric swatches, ticket fragments, and thin stone tablets arranged around a miniature Cardea doorway. Each object contains only abstract marks, color fields, and material clues, never readable text. A gentle orbital braid groups related pieces into constellations, while one hand calmly removes a card from the cluster, expressing that memory is visible, editable, and removable.
Composition: intimate top-down still life; cluster weighted toward the lower-right; reserve the upper-left for live heading and copy; maintain clear separation between objects so real interactive note components can align with the scene
Style: museum archive photography translated into soft risograph and editorial collage, refined Japanese stationery sensibility, tactile paper and linen
Palette: warm bone foundation, Cardea blue cards, coral indexing tabs, orbital-black linework, restrained secondary domain colors only inside small cards
Lighting/mood: soft northern daylight, personal and trustworthy
Constraints: no readable writing, no fake UI, no Post-it branding, no clutter, no logo, no watermark
Avoid: scrapbook kitsch, occult tarot, productivity stock photography, glowing brain imagery, data-cloud iconography, glossy plastic
```

## 4. Authority: Cardea pauses at the hinge

Placement: approvals and safety section, paired with a real approval component and spending-envelope card.

Desktop target: 16:9 close composition, with focal action on the left and open space on the right for live UI.

Prompt:

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: Cardea landing-page authority and approval section
Primary request: Show Cardea's sculptural hand resting near a coral hinge-orb on the edge of a monumental blue doorway. The door is open only a few degrees and a thin orbital-black line pauses exactly at the hinge, visually communicating that Cardea can prepare everything but the human controls irreversible passage. Through the narrow opening, reveal only a sliver of warm light and organized objects waiting beyond.
Composition: extreme editorial detail; hand and hinge occupy the left 45 percent; reserve the right 45 percent as clean warm-bone space for a real approval panel; crop boldly so the image feels architectural rather than illustrative
Style: classical marble detail reinterpreted as two-color screenprint with one coral registration mark, crisp geometry, tactile paper grain
Palette: warm bone, Cardea blue, hinge coral focal point, orbital black line
Lighting/mood: controlled, reassuring, consequential
Constraints: anatomically correct hand, no payment-card numbers, no currency symbols, no text, no interface, no keys, no lock icon, no watermark
Avoid: fintech advertisement, Michelangelo imitation, robotic hand, handshake, security shield, glowing button, glossy 3D
```

## 5. Closing: The next world is ready

Placement: final CTA and footer transition, with a dark-mode counterpart for theme continuity.

Desktop target: 16:9 landscape, central doorway low-middle, large negative space for closing copy.

Prompt:

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: Cardea landing-page closing CTA panorama
Primary request: Create a nocturnal counterpart to the hero. A small Cardea silhouette stands before an enormous open threshold floating in orbital-black space. Through it appears a warm-bone horizon with one restrained Cardea-blue path leading forward. Her single braided orbit crosses the scene and resolves into a hinge-coral point at the doorway, suggesting the mission is ready when the user chooses to enter.
Composition: wide cinematic landscape with the doorway centered slightly below midline; preserve the upper 45 percent as calm dark negative space for live closing copy; keep all detail away from footer navigation zones
Style: editorial celestial mural, matte screenprint ink on near-black paper, classical scale with modern transit precision
Palette: orbital black #11110F dominant, warm bone portal light, Cardea blue path, one hinge-coral point
Lighting/mood: quiet confidence and possibility, not dramatic science fiction
Constraints: no text, no logo, no interface, no starscape clutter, no watermark; must remain legible with reduced contrast and in a static image
Avoid: space opera, astronaut imagery, lens flare, neon portal, purple galaxy, cinematic movie-poster clichés, generic AI horizon
```

## Production notes for the landing-page agent

1. Generate the hero first and lock the asset family from that result before generating the remaining four.
2. Curate three hero variants, choose one, then carry its exact pigment, grain, Cardea anatomy, arch geometry, and line weight into every later prompt.
3. Keep the actual Cardea canvas, website previews, memory notes, approval cards, and dependency graph as real components. Generated images provide atmosphere only.
4. Create separate mobile prompts and crops after desktop composition is approved.
5. Record model/provider, prompt, date, references, selected output, and edits for provenance.
6. Export optimized AVIF or WebP plus static fallback, and respect reduced-motion preferences when images are animated or parallaxed.
